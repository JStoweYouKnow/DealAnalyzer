Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 29, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to The Comfort Finder ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and web services (collectively, the "Service").

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Email address, name, and authentication credentials when you create an account
  • Property Data: Property addresses, purchase prices, rental income, expenses, and other real estate investment data you input
  • Documents: Property listing emails, PDFs, spreadsheets, and photos you upload for analysis
  • Investment Criteria: Your preferred investment parameters and search criteria

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: Features used, pages viewed, time spent in the app, crash reports
  • Location Data: Approximate location derived from IP address (not precise GPS location)
  • Cookies: Session tokens and authentication cookies

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • OAuth Providers: If you sign in with Google or other providers, we receive your email and basic profile information
  • Gmail Integration: If you connect Gmail, we access property listing emails you authorize

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain our Service
  • Authenticate your account and maintain security
  • Analyze property investments using our AI-powered tools
  • Store your property analyses and investment criteria
  • Send you email notifications about your analyses (if you opt-in)
  • Improve and optimize our Service
  • Provide customer support
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Where We Store Data

  • Clerk: User authentication and session management (encrypted, SOC 2 compliant)
  • Convex: Database for property analyses and user data (encrypted at rest and in transit)
  • Vercel: Application hosting (encrypted HTTPS connections)
  • Your Device: Temporary cache for offline functionality

4.2 Security Measures

  • End-to-end encryption for data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Encrypted data storage
  • Secure authentication via Clerk (industry-standard OAuth 2.0)
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Access controls limiting data access to authorized personnel only

Note: No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we implement industry-standard security measures, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

5.1 Third-Party Service Providers

We share your data with trusted service providers who help us operate our Service:

  • Clerk (Authentication): Clerk Privacy Policy
  • Convex (Database): Convex Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI (AI Analysis): OpenAI Privacy Policy
  • Stripe (Payments, if applicable): Stripe Privacy Policy

5.2 We Do NOT Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. Period.

5.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to:

  • Valid legal process (subpoena, court order)
  • Protection of our rights, property, or safety
  • Investigation of fraud or security issues
  • Compliance with regulatory obligations

6. Your Privacy Rights

6.1 Access and Control

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Export: Download your property analyses and data
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from email communications

6.2 How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at:

  • Email: privacy@comfortfinder.com
  • In-App: Settings → Privacy → Delete Account

6.3 Data Retention

  • Active Accounts: We retain your data while your account is active
  • Deleted Accounts: Data is permanently deleted within 30 days of account deletion
  • Legal Requirements: Some data may be retained longer to comply with legal obligations

7. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 in Europe). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

8. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place through:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) with service providers
  • Service providers' compliance with GDPR and other privacy frameworks

9. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act:

  • Right to know what personal information we collect
  • Right to delete your personal information
  • Right to opt-out of sale of personal information (we don't sell data)
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights

10. European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have additional rights under GDPR:

  • Right to data portability
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to object to processing
  • Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

11. Cookies and Tracking

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for authentication and core functionality
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how you use the Service
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences

You can control cookies through your browser settings, but this may affect Service functionality.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes via:

  • Email to your registered address

  • Prominent notice on our website

Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
